Questions

Where are my colonies saved? Could I lose them?

On your iPhone or iPad. Every colony saves itself every 20 seconds and whenever you leave the app, and it is kept twice: in the app's own storage, and as files in the app's private folder, which iOS includes in your iCloud Backup or computer backup. If one copy is ever lost, the colony comes back from the other the next time the app opens.

For extra peace of mind, export a colony now and then (Settings → Export save) and keep the file in Files or iCloud Drive.

How do I move my colonies to a new iPhone or iPad?

Set up the new device from a backup of the old one (iCloud, a computer, or Quick Start), and your colonies come across with the app.

Or move them yourself: on the old device open the colony and choose Settings → Export save, send the file (AirDrop, Files or Mail), then on the new device choose Settings → Import save and pick it. It arrives as a new colony, exactly where it was.

Does the colony keep living when I close the app?

No. It waits, paused, exactly where you left it, however long you are away. When you come back, a welcome card shows where things stand, and nothing happens until you tap Resume. If you were only gone a minute or two, it just carries on.

I can't hear anything.

Sounds start after your first tap in the game. Check that your iPhone isn't in silent mode (the switch or the Action button on the side), and that Settings → Sound is on in the game. There is no music, by design: only the land, the nests and the ants, so you can play your own music alongside.

My device gets warm, or the battery drains quickly.

Running a living colony is real work, and speed multiplies it: at 25× a colony day passes every half second. Play at 1× to 5× when you are just watching, and pause when you put the device down. The game stops its clock and saves whenever you switch to another app.

How do I start another colony, or go back to an old one?

Open My colonies (under More on iPhone, or the button with stacked boxes in the bottom bar on iPad). Start a new colony there, open any colony you have kept, or rename, copy, export or delete one. Every colony carries on from where you left it.

Deleting a colony takes it out of your list, but a recovery copy stays on your device, under Other recovery copies in My colonies, so you can bring it back if you change your mind. To remove everything the app keeps, delete the app.

Can a friend play on the same island?

Yes. Every island is grown from a seed. On the map, the island's card shows its size and seed: tap Copy and send the seed to your friend. They start a new colony of the same species, paste it into Island seed and pick the same size, and the same island grows for them. What their colony does there is its own story.

What is the difference between Observer and Hive Mind?

Observer is for watching: the automatic feeder keeps the colony fed, everything is visible, and the colony makes its own choices. Hive Mind puts you in charge: you feed the colony, learn about its neighbours only as its scouts find them, make its choices and can chase optional goals. Switch any time in Settings; nothing is reset.

The colony is in trouble. What can I do?

The colony panel lists what matters right now (protein running low, a crowded farm, a nest under attack) and taps through to where it is happening. It often suggests an order too, such as Forage when food runs low or Defend during a raid. Feed by hand, plant trees on the colony's land, or as Hive Mind send food or workers to a struggling nest. If the royal line dies out completely, a new queen arrives some days later and founds a new dynasty in the old nest.

Why has my colony gone quiet in winter?

It is resting, as real ants do. Through winter, garden, wood, carpenter and harvester ants stay deep in the nest: the queen stops laying and the workers hardly go out. The season on screen reads Winter · hibernating, and the colony panel says so too. They wake up in spring. To play without winters, turn off Settings → Seasons and winter hibernation.

Is my data collected?

No. Ant Laboratory has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your colonies never leave your device unless you export them. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with the Apple Account you bought the app with.
